# Terminology Contract

Inherited from the StreetView-Multimodal-Framework charter (2026-08-26) and binding for all
PyRestore code, notebooks, reports and presentations.

## Confirmed terms

| Term | Use | Do not imply |
|---|---|---|
| Physical measurement | A quantity produced by a declared CV operation | Field truth unless independently validated |
| Semantic interpretation | A schema-constrained VLM judgement | Direct measurement of human experience |
| Visible evidence | Image content cited in support of an interpretation | Faithful access to hidden model reasoning |
| Condition assessment | Description of visible state in one scene | That change or renewal occurred |
| Change detection | Identification of differences between comparable observations | That the difference is a planned intervention |
| Urban-renewal detection | Classification of substantive before/after intervention evidence | Causal impact, justice or policy effectiveness |
| Perceived-impact estimate | Modelled change in a defined perception construct | Observed resident response without human data |
| Confounder / nuisance change | A visual difference unrelated to the target phenomenon | A minor implementation detail that may be ignored |
| Operational portability | The same software contract runs on another data package | Scientific validity transfers automatically |
| Validation | Comparison against a reference appropriate to a specific claim | Generic proof that all outputs are correct |
| Restorative environment / restorative experience | ART-grounded perceptual construct | "Urban recovery" or urban resilience |
| Analysis-ready | Harmonised, georeferenced, quality-recorded indicator table | That any accuracy claim is automatically warranted |

## Prohibited shortcuts

- Do not call a single-image condition score an urban-renewal result.
- Do not call VLM-generated text a causal explanation without a faithfulness experiment.
- Do not call reference-model agreement ground truth unless the reference is actually manual truth.
- Do not call spatial clustering a causal intervention priority.
- Do not use "CV+VLM fusion" unless the operation and evaluated benefit are explicitly defined.
- Do not report a global Moran's I as "significant" without its permutation p-value.
- Do not describe LISA Low-Low clusters as measured health outcomes or confirmed intervention sites.
